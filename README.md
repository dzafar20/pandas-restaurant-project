# Restaurant Orders Analysis 🍽️

Analyzing 3 months of restaurant order data to find sales patterns, busiest hours, and revenue insights.

## 🔍 What I analyzed
- Best and worst selling menu items by revenue
- Busiest hours of the day (lunch peak vs dinner peak)
- Revenue patterns by hour and day of week (heatmap)
- Italian-only items revenue ranking

## 🛠️ Tools used
- Python
- pandas (data manipulation)
- matplotlib (charts)
- seaborn (heatmap)

## 📊 Key findings
- Lunch (12-1 PM) is the busiest hour, beating dinner
- 3 PM is the slowest hour — opportunity for happy hour promotions
